MyPulse Privacy Policy

MyPulse is a wellness reference app that uses the rear camera and flashlight on your iPhone to estimate heart rate. This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored, how camera access is used, and how you can control local records.

Last updated: July 24, 2026 Applies to: MyPulse iOS beta Data is stored locally by default
MyPulse is not a medical device. Measurement results are for general wellness tracking and lifestyle reference only.

1. Information We Store

The current version does not create user accounts and does not require you to sign in. The app may store the following information on your device:

  • Estimated heart rate values, such as beats per minute.
  • Measurement time, measurement tags, notes, signal quality, confidence score, and measurement status.
  • App preferences such as privacy mode and local acknowledgement of safety notices.

This information is used to show your latest result, local history, 7-day summary, and resting reference values inside the app.

2. Camera and Flashlight Use

MyPulse uses the rear camera and flashlight to read color changes in your fingertip and estimate pulse rhythm and heart rate. Camera frames are used for real-time analysis only.

  • The app does not save photos.
  • The app does not save videos.
  • The app does not upload camera frames to a server.
  • If you deny camera permission, measurement features will not work, but you can still view existing local records and settings.

3. Where Data Is Stored

In the current version, measurement records are stored locally on your device by default. The current version does not include accounts, cloud sync, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or a remote health database.

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, analytics, crash diagnostics, or other network features, we will update this Privacy Policy to explain the relevant data types, purposes, sharing practices, user choices, and platform privacy disclosures.

4. How We Use Information

Local records may be used for the following purposes:

  • Displaying your most recent measurement.
  • Generating recent averages, high and low values, and valid measurement counts.
  • Estimating a personal resting reference based on resting measurements.
  • Showing recheck suggestions based on confidence score and signal quality.

In the current version, we do not sell, rent, or disclose your measurement records to third parties.

5. Deletion and Controls

You can clear local measurement records from the app's Settings or History screen. Once deleted, the app cannot restore those records. You can also revoke camera permission in iOS Settings. If camera permission is revoked, the app cannot perform camera-based measurements.

If you uninstall the app, iOS typically removes data stored by the app on your device. This behavior may depend on your iOS version, backups, and restore settings.

6. Data Security

We reduce privacy risk by minimizing data collection. The current version is designed without accounts, without uploading measurement records, without saving photos or videos, and without third-party tracking.

Please note that local data may still be affected by device loss, jailbreaking, shared device access, system backups, or other people accessing your device. We recommend using a device passcode and being careful when sharing screenshots or screen recordings.

7. Children's Privacy

MyPulse is intended as a general wellness reference tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Minors should use the app only with guidance from a parent or guardian. The app should not be used to evaluate the health of children, infants, or newborns.

8. Third-Party Services

The current version does not include advertising, third-party analytics, cloud sync, or remote health data services. If third-party services are added later, this Privacy Policy will describe the provider, data purpose, sharing scope, and available choices.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy due to changes in product features, laws, platform requirements, or operational practices. When updated, the “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
